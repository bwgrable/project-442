    What this engine study simplifies

    This is an illustrative V8 bottom end inspired by Project442's Rocket400 theme. Geometry, bank offsets and crank phases teach relationships; they are not verified historical dimensions, cylinder numbering or a firing order.

    The block is prepared. Rings accompany each piston; wrist pins, rod bearings and matched rod caps accompany each connecting rod. Main bearing lower shells accompany the main caps. The oil pump and pickup are a representative assembly; pump drive, oil flow and combustion are not simulated.

    Connected pistons move with the supported crank. Unconnected pistons are held at rest for teaching. The pan closes the underside after all internal connections are complete. Cutaway opens walls for inspection. Heads, valves and service procedures are outside this study.
