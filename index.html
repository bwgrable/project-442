    About this engine study

    This is a focused one-cylinder section inspired by a V8 rotating assembly. The block is prepared. Rings, bearings and matching caps are included with their parent parts. Selectable connections teach how parts relate; they are not a factory assembly sequence.

    Geometry and proportions are illustrative, not verified Rocket 400 dimensions or repair instructions. Other cylinders, combustion, valves and service procedures are outside this pilot.
