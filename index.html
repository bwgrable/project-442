What this small experiment simplifies

Parts move across a horizontal work plane. Roll is adjustable; axial orientation and final seating height are preset. Bearings, rings and wrist pin are prepared. Main caps move as a set; connections stand in for fastening. This is illustrative geometry, not factory dimensions or a service procedure. Other cylinders are outside this experiment.

Touch: one finger moves a part or orbits empty space. A second finger cancels a part drag; lift both fingers, then pinch to zoom. Scroll using the page outside the 3D view.
