What this small experiment simplifies

The block, crankshaft, main caps, bearings and piston rings are prepared. Your piston, rod and wrist pin begin separate. Parts lie on a horizontal inspection plane, with axial orientation prepared; an aligned pin slides only along its axis. Roll is adjustable. Final cylinder orientation and seating height are preset. The rod cap represents the final crank connection. There is no collision, force or fastening simulation. Geometry is illustrative, not factory dimensions or a service procedure. Other cylinders are outside this experiment.

Touch: one finger moves a part or orbits empty space. A second finger cancels a part drag; lift both fingers, then pinch to zoom. Scroll using the page outside the 3D view.
